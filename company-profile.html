Our Installation Process

Every installation follows a disciplined, staged process — refined over major regional vertical transport installations since 2021 — that keeps quality consistent, prevents site snags, and keeps handover timelines strictly predictable:

  • 01
    Shaft preparation and template installation — Verification of civil pit, overhead clearances, plumb line drop, and top/bottom template alignment.
  • 02
    Plumb reading, bracket fixing, and rail alignment — Laser alignment and precision fixing of counterweight and car guide rails to tight tolerances.
  • 03
    Entrance fixing and motor hoisting — Landing door header assembly, sill alignment, machine-room bedplate rigging, and gearless traction motor hoisting.
  • 04
    Electrical work, trunking, and controller fixing — Shaft wiring, traveling cables, landing call stations, and microprocessor controller cabinet termination.
  • 05
    Cabin work and roping — Car sling assembly, platform shock mounting, safety gear adjustment, car wall panels, and high-tensile steel wire roping.
  • 06
    Slow-speed and high-speed commissioning — Inspection drive testing, safety circuit verification, door operator tuning, and full-speed ride comfort calibration.
  • 07
    Final TÜV / load testing and handover — Full static/dynamic buffer load tests, emergency battery rescue (ARD) validation, and Civil Defence sign-off.
Execution Benchmark: A typical 3-stop, sub-800kg elevator installation is completed in approximately 17 working days. Larger installations — such as 10-stop elevators with 60–70m shaft heights — are delivered in around 1.5 months.

Occupational Health & Safety (OSH) Policy

Al Dirayah Electromechanical Services LLC is strictly committed to providing a safe, incident-free, and healthy working environment for all employees, subcontractors, and site visitors. Our operations comply fully with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on the Regulation of Labour Relations, Cabinet Resolution No. 33 of 2022 concerning Occupational Health and Safety, and the Abu Dhabi Occupational Safety and Health System Framework (OSHAD-SF).

  • Site Hazard Assessment: Identifying and evaluating workplace hazards for every elevator shaft and machine room prior to commencement, implementing engineering controls to eliminate risk.
  • Mandatory PPE Compliance: Supplying all technicians with certified personal protective equipment, including dual-lanyard safety harnesses, EN 397 helmets, cut-resistant gloves, and safety footwear.
  • Task-Specific Safety Training: Delivering mandatory safety induction, working-at-height certifications, scaffolding safety, and confined-space training for all technicians prior to deployment.
  • Documented RAMS: Developing and maintaining approved Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS) for each installation and rigging stage.
  • Incident Investigation & Reporting: Immediate reporting, root-cause analysis, and corrective action tracking for all incidents and near-misses in accordance with statutory guidelines.
  • Routine Site Audits: Conducting weekly internal safety inspections and unannounced HSE compliance audits across all active installation sites.
  • Client & Consultant HSE Alignment: Full adherence to main contractor site safety rules, permit-to-work (PTW) protocols, and consultant specifications.
  • Continuous Policy Review: Periodic review and updating of our OSH policy framework to incorporate new UAE statutory mandates and international engineering best practices.

Responsibility for OSH implementation rests with executive management, supervised on-site by dedicated Project Engineers and Site Safety Officers, with every team member empowered to stop work if conditions are deemed unsafe.
