    Which villa lift is right for you?

    Traction, hydraulic and pneumatic options to fit your villa layout, whether you are building new or retrofitting.

    01

    MRL Gearless Villa Lifts

    No machine room needed. Quiet enough to run next to a bedroom (about 40 dB).

    02

    Low-Overhead Hydraulic Lifts

    Ideal for retrofitting into existing residential structures. Requires only a shallow 200mm pit and minimal top floor overhead, featuring smooth hydraulic proportional valve leveling.

    03

    360° Panoramic Glass Cabins

    Curved circular or polygonal laminated safety glass enclosures with champagne gold stainless steel structure. Works well in the middle of a staircase.

    04

    Pitless Pneumatic Vacuum Lifts

    Air-driven self-supporting cylindrical elevators. Installs directly onto existing villa floor slabs without structural shaft excavation or pit drilling.

    05

    Custom Italian Interior Fit-Outs

    Marble, leather, bronze mirror or LED ceiling: we match your interior designer’s spec.

    06

    Power-cut rescue

    Brings the lift to the nearest floor and opens the doors automatically if the power fails.
